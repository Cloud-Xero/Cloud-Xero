## § 1 &nbsp; About

I’m an independent software engineer based in **Tokyo, Japan**. From web apps to workflow automation, development foundations, and technical advisory, I support teams that have few or no engineers of their own.

I work directly with marketers, sales teams, and the people doing the work to pin down requirements, then carry it through design, implementation, and deployment. What I value most is leaving behind systems that keep running without me.

<br/>

<a id="what-i-do"></a>

## § 2 &nbsp; What I do

**1.1 &nbsp; Web application development**<br/>
From requirements to design, implementation, and operations, mainly with Next.js and Astro.

**1.2 &nbsp; Automation & DX**<br/>
Reducing manual work with GAS, Teams bots, and internal tools, in a form the team can keep running.

**1.3 &nbsp; Development foundations & AI**<br/>
Templates, CI/CD, and IaC, plus Claude Code workflows that standardize how a team builds.

**1.4 &nbsp; Technical advisory & mentoring**<br/>
Choosing technology for teams without engineers, and mentoring people until they can work on their own.
